# Pillars

## Pitch

You are a **hauler** on a tidally locked exoplanet. A thin chain of settlements
clings to the twilight band between a scorched dayside and a frozen nightside.
The colony's supply chain is broken, its comms network is a handful of unlinked
islands, and nobody is coming to help.

You drive a rover. You carry cargo. You extend the network. You find out what
this planet is.

Non-combat, traversal-first, hard science fiction.

## Core loop

1. **Accept** a contract at a settlement
2. **Load** cargo - mass, volume, fragility and balance all matter
3. **Traverse** - manage power, O₂, heat, dose, terrain, flare risk
4. **Deliver** - the *condition* of the cargo sets the payout and standing
5. **Spend** on upgrades and field structures
6. **Extend** [[The-Lattice]], **hop** [[The-Lander]], repeat at higher difficulty

## Hard nos

Written down so we don't drift.

1. **No combat.** The environment is the antagonist. If a problem wants a
   weapon as its answer, the problem is wrong.
2. **The world is not a checklist.** No icon vomit. [[The-Lattice]] reveals
   *terrain*, not collectibles.
3. **Physics over stats.** [[Cargo]] behaves. It is never just a number in a
   menu.
4. **Hard sci-fi look.** Machined, bolted, insulated, worn. No glowing
   holograms without a stated in-world reason. See [[Visual-Direction]].
5. **Silence is a feature.** 18 kPa carries sound, thinly. Use that.

## Lineage, and where we diverge

Death Stranding is the obvious parent: cargo as the verb, a hostile crossing,
characters met at the ends of routes. The divergences that have to carry their
own weight:

| Death Stranding | Here |
|---|---|
| Timefall ages things | [[Flares]] irradiate and degrade - a warned, survivable event |
| Chiral network | [[The-Lattice]] - line-of-sight relays, so terrain occlusion is real |
| Fixed home base | [[The-Lander]] - a hopper you commit fuel to move |
| Supernatural threat (BTs) | Nothing. Environmental pressure and a xenological mystery |
