# Session 2026-09-01

Mac dropped two 8193x8193 Gaea exports into `game/assets/terrain/` and asked
whether they could replace the procedural terrain, keeping the noise available
for testing. They could. The swap itself was small; most of the session went on
the things the swap broke.

## Changed in the editor (Mac)

- Delivered `world_01_height.exr` and `world_01_color.tif` - 420 MB together.

## Changed in code (Claude)

- **`tools/bake-terrain.py`.** Turns the masters into the ~19 MB the game
  loads. Implements its own EXR read and write, because OpenEXR is a heavy
  build dependency for what is zlib plus a byte shuffle, and because the TIFF
  has to be re-encoded regardless - Godot cannot import TIFF at all.
- **`terrain.gd` gained a second source.** `height_source` picks Heightmap or
  Procedural; `height_at()`, `world_height_at()` and `rebuilt` behave
  identically either way, so [[Scatter]], [[The-Lattice]] and the facilities
  never learn which is in use. Procedural is still the *script* default, so no
  test that builds its own patch pays for the map; the world scene sets
  Heightmap explicitly.
- **The mesh now writes a real UV2**, spanning the patch 0..1, and
  `painterly.gdshader` samples the macro albedo through it. UV1 is world metres
  over 16 and tiles hundreds of times - deriving one from the other would have
  hard-coded the patch size into the material.
- **`test_world.gd` settles facilities and relays onto the ground**, by group,
  the way it already did for crates.
- **`rock_scatter.gd` scatters around the terrain's actual position** instead of
  around the world origin.
- New: `test_heightmap_terrain`, `terrain_capture`, `probe_heightmap_import`,
  `probe_world_placement`.

## Broke / noticed

- **World origin landed on a 42 degree face at 204 m.** The map's peak sits at
  its centre, and that is exactly where the astronaut, the rover and every
  crate spawn. Fixed with a terrain offset of (-470, 0, +1242), which puts a
  0.19 degree playa under the origin and leaves the massif 1.3 km off and
  prominent. One number on the node; Mac can move it.
- **The Hearth ended up 13.6 m underground**, Longshadow and the ridge relay
  with it. All three carried Y values hand-tuned against the procedural patch.
  Every headless test passed throughout - the nodes existed and were exactly
  where they had been told to be. Only `probe_world_placement` caught it, which
  is why that probe now exists.
- **The albedo was importing unmipmapped**, because `detect_3d/compress_to`
  had not fired yet. A 4096 map over 4096 m with no mipmaps aliases at distance
  and crawls under camera motion. Both `.import` files are now set by hand -
  and the *heightfield* has detect_3d disarmed, because a lossy re-import would
  silently change the shape of the world.
- **Godot expanded the 218 MB height master into an 805 MB texture cache** just
  by having it sit in `res://`. The masters now live under a `.gdignore`.
- Faint soft-edged rectangles on the near ground in the captures. They are
  present in the pre-existing look-dev shots too, so they are not from this
  work - most likely the brush-stamp rung ladder or a shadow split boundary.
  Worth a look when the next look-dev pass happens.

## Next

- Colour grading on the macro albedo. The master is pinker and more saturated
  than [[Visual-Direction]] asks for; `macro_tint` and `macro_saturation` move
  it without a re-bake, and nobody has judged where they should sit.
- The spawn playa is deliberately the flattest ground on the map, which makes
  the immediate area bland. Moving the offset trades that against character.
- `ProceduralTerrain` is now half a lie as a class name. Renaming touches
  sixteen test files, so it is a deliberate TODO rather than drift.

## Notes updated

- [[Terrain]] - rewritten. `verified` bumped to 2026-09-01, still `built`.
- [[Decision-Log]] - the 4096 m footprint, the 210 m relief, straight albedo
  replacement, and masters staying out of git.
- `CLAUDE.md` - four new verified engine facts, the new test and capture
  commands, and the masters path.

# Session 2026-09-01 (second half)

Mac asked for gizmos to control the spawn points that scripts were placing, so
elements can be positioned precisely now that there is a heightmap to position
them against. Three problems were hiding inside that one request.

## Changed in code (Claude)

- **New `SpawnPoint` marker** (`scripts/world/spawn_point.gd`). A plain `Node3D`
  with an `spawn_id`, a `ground_clearance` and a yaw. `test_world.gd` resolves
  `astronaut`, `rover` and `beacon` through it instead of the `Vector3` literals
  it used to carry, and falls back to those literals when a scene has no marker.
- **New editor addon** (`addons/spawn_gizmos/`) - the project's first. An
  `EditorNode3DGizmoPlugin` draws each marker as a stake, contact ring, facing
  arrow and `TextMesh` id, with a draggable handle for the clearance; a snapper
  re-solves height while the selection is dragged; a **Snap to ground** button
  covers the case the drag cannot, a node gone stale because the terrain moved.
- **`ground_clearance` on `Facility`, `Relay` and `Crate`.** Plain exports -
  none of the three became `@tool`. The property is what the editor tooling
  recognises, so a new node opts in by declaring it rather than by being added
  to a list somewhere.
- **`terrain.gd` gained `is_built()`**, and `height_at_index()` no longer
  indexes an empty array. The snapper refuses to write until a terrain is built,
  because an unbuilt one answers zero for every point and zero is a plausible
  height.
- **`test_world.gd` settles cargo by group**, not by walking `$Crates`.
- New: `test_spawn_points`.

## Broke / noticed

- **The scene file was lying by metres.** The Hearth's stored Y was **7 m** off
  the ground beneath it and the recovered mast **6 m**, three weeks after the
  terrain swap that caused it and with every headless test passing. Both are now
  seeded from a solved height, and the editor keeps them there.
- **The recovered mast was never being settled at all.** `_settle_crates()`
  walked the children of `$Crates`, and `Mast105` lives under `$LooseCargo` - so
  it was the one crate in the world still keeping a hand-tuned Y. Found by
  switching the walk to the `cargo` group.
- **A `Node` built with `.new()` and never parented is never freed.** Three
  lines probing the unbuilt-terrain path leaked, and the report arrived at exit
  as RID and ObjectDB errors naming engine internals rather than the test.
- **Gizmo code cannot be tested from a game run.**
  `EditorNode3DGizmoPlugin.new()` fails outside the editor, and because a test
  scene whose script errors never quits, the attempt *hung* for five minutes
  rather than failing. Verified instead by printing from the plugin's
  `_enter_tree()` under `--headless --editor`.
