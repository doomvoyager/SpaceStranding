### Findings from the proposal round, for the work queued next

- **The Moon alone will make the rover faster, not slower.** Nothing caps speed
  today, and the last gravity change went the other way (29 m -> 7 m in 10 s).
- **Mass cannot be tuned live**: `refresh_load()` rewrites it from a value
  captured at `_ready`. And in `VehicleBody3D`, suspension and grip scale with
  mass, so mass alone only makes the rover sluggish.
- From Godot's source, **to be probed**: `max_engine_force` applies per driven
  wheel (1170 is 7,020 N), and the brake is a per-wheel, per-tick impulse.
- **Horizon arithmetic**: 2.43 km from a 1.7 m eye, but the ground drops only
  1.7 m at 2.4 km and 22 m at a 3x3 corner against 210 m of relief - so it culls
  small things, not hills, and vacuum has no fog to hide the world's edge.
- **At a sixth of the gravity, bumps behave the same at ~0.55x today's speed**,
  and braking distances roughly triple. "Tips before it slides" is not a lunar
  fact: gravity cancels out of it, and `wheel_friction_slip` decides it.
- **No recorded decision rules out first person.** The cab box is about 0.77 m
  deck to roof, too low for a seated eye - Mac's blockout call.
