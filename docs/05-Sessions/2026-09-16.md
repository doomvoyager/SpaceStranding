## Broke / noticed

- **Physics could not have done the occlusion.** The streamed ground has no
  collision past 1 km and the wheels have no shapes; the picture has both.
  The probe measured it before the proposal went to Mac.
- **The port did not compile in Godot 4 and graded the whole frame** even with
  no sun in it. Its broad halo, at its own weight, washed the frame orange and
  doubled its brightness looking at the sun; weighted apart, it reads as a
  lens.
- **Dark dust is invisible against a black sky.** The first pass moved 0.8% of
  a shaded frame at full coverage. Lit by the frame's mean, 4.4%.
- **A node-typed export in a hand-written scene came through null** - the
  `node_paths` header. Both scripts use the project's `*_path` idiom now, and
  `LensDust` finds its vehicle's WheelDust without a path.
- **A uniform named like a function whited out every capture frame.** Headless
  would have caught it - measured: the dummy renderer parses shaders too.
- **Wall-clock frame time could not see the glass's cost**; the viewport GPU
  timer read 0.019 ms. All three facts are in `CLAUDE.md`.
- The Debug-Panel note's target table had been missing Wheel dust, Wheel
  tracks and Footprints since the 14th; fixed with the new row.
- The Shadertoy page for the flare was behind a bot check; the licence is from
  the godotshaders port (MIT) and a search summary of the original.
