## Broke / noticed

- Four bugs were caught by review before first run, all invisible without the
  engine: terrain triangle **winding** (Godot wants clockwise; the world would
  have been backface-culled and unwalkable), `HeightMapShape3D` needing a
  non-uniform scale **Jolt rejects**, a `seed` variable shadowing GDScript's
  built-in `seed()`, and `E` reaching both astronaut and rover in one frame.
- `project.godot` targeted 4.4; the engine is 4.7.1. Corrected.
- The star sits at 5.5°, not the intended 4°, because grazing directional light
  wrecks shadow maps. Noted in [[Visual-Direction]].

## Art direction (later the same day)

Mac brought four of their own space paintings and asked for a stylized
painterly look. Render style was genuinely unsettled - [[Visual-Direction]]
covered palette, materials and the fixed star but never *how* things are drawn.

- Mac chose **saturated** over the doc's muted palette, and **surface treatment
  only** (no post pass). Both recorded in [[Decision-Log]];
  [[Visual-Direction]] rewritten to match, including what it changes about
  modelling.
- Built `res://shaders/painterly.gdshader` - banded diffuse with a custom
  `light()`, hue-shifted shadow, no specular, fresnel rim, triplanar brush
  stamps. Presets in `res://materials/`. `terrain.gd` gained an exported
  `surface_material` defaulting to the regolith preset, so nothing in Mac's
  scene had to be touched.
- Added `res://tests/look_dev_capture.tscn`, which boots the real world and
  writes stills from fixed vantage points to `user://look_dev/`. Must run
  windowed - `--headless` is the dummy renderer and produces no image.

## Broke / noticed (art pass)

Three failures, all found by rendering rather than by reasoning:

- `AMBIENT_LIGHT` does not exist in `fragment()` or `light()` on 4.7.1. Probed
  both. Shader now disables engine ambient and carries its own fill.
- **Engine ambient erased the banding entirely** - terrain rendered as an
  untouched smooth CG gradient while the flat-faced test boxes banded fine,
  which is what gave it away.
- **Hard bands turned the soft-shadow penumbra into a loud ordered stipple.**
  Chased it to volumetric fog first (wrong - disabling it changed nothing), then
  to the real cause: folding `ATTENUATION` into the value being quantised
  thresholds Godot's per-pixel PCSS filter noise. Both this and the
  `AMBIENT_LIGHT` fact are now in `CLAUDE.md`.

Also noticed: brush stamps at a fixed world size alias into moire at any
distance. Fixed with a power-of-two distance ladder that keeps cell size
roughly constant on screen while staying world-locked, so they should not swim.
**Should** - nobody has driven it yet.
