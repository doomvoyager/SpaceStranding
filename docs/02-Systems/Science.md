# Science

A parallel currency to money, and the carrier of the story.

## Behaviour

Sampling, surveying and investigating anomalies earn **research**, which gates
the deepest tier of [[Progression]].

The narrative thread is xenological: what happened to the first expedition, and
what this planet actually is. **Revealed through what you find in the field,
not through cutscenes.**
